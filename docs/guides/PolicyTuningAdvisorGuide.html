DLP Policy Tuning Advisor Guide

Use deterministic evidence to prioritize policy review without sending alert data anywhere or automatically deciding whether an alert is a false positive.

1. Prepare an export

Export Forcepoint DLP incidents as CSV or XLSX. Include Policies, Source, Destination, File Name, Violation Triggers, Channel, Application, ID, and Incident Time where available. Multiple files can be selected and are combined.

2. Interpret the score

The Tuning Candidate Score ranks policies for review. Points come from visible findings such as dominant trigger sets, repeated signatures, ten-minute bursts, concentrated destinations, repeated templates, and multi-user patterns. Small datasets receive a confidence adjustment. High, Medium, and Low describe tuning opportunity—not incident legitimacy.

3. Review evidence

Expand a finding to see why it was detected, its statistics, the suggested configuration or workflow area, and every contributing alert. Confirm business ownership and destination approval before creating an exception. Prefer narrowly scoped exceptions and validate the result in Forcepoint.

Privacy and limitations

Parsing and analysis run locally in the browser with fixed rules; no AI analysis is performed. The advisor infers patterns from export values and cannot know business authorization. An internal-looking destination is explicitly not treated as proof of approval, and the tool never labels alerts as false positives.
